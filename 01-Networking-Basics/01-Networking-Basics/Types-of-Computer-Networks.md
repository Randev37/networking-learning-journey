# Types of Computer Networks

## 1. PAN (Personal Area Network)
A PAN connects personal devices within a short distance.

Example: Connecting a smartphone to a smartwatch using Bluetooth.

## 2. LAN (Local Area Network)
A LAN connects devices within a limited geographical area.

Example: A university computer laboratory.

## 3. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
A MAN connects networks across a city or metropolitan area.

Example: A network connecting multiple branches across a city.

## 4. WAN (Wide Area Network)
A WAN connects networks over large geographical areas.

Example: The Internet.
